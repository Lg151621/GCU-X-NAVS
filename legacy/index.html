Who we are

Not just hearers of the Word. Doers.

The Navigators started in 1933 with one man discipling sailors in the U.S. Navy. The idea hasn't changed: one life invested in another, who invests in another. That's what we're about at GCU.

For parents

Who your student is spending time with.

Sending a student off to college is a big step. Here's what you should know about us.

Who are The Navigators?

A nondenominational Christian ministry founded in 1933, with staff on college campuses, military bases, and in communities across the U.S. and around the world.

Who leads the GCU group?

Cam and Emma Kessner direct the ministry, alongside staff members Emily and Elyse. Student leaders are trained and mentored by staff.

What does a typical week look like?

A Monday evening gathering, an optional Friday morning prayer walk, and an outreach trip to ASU every other Friday. Students also meet in small groups and one-on-one with a mentor.

How do I reach someone?

Email the staff at staff email. We're always glad to talk.
