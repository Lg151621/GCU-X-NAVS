# /resources: binder fidelity audit

Audited 2026-10-29 against **Discipleship Binder (ALL) - 2025 (4).pdf** (75 PDF pages, 27,360,463 bytes,
SHA-256 `9617f6d20ec236454c90ea33f781773eb97fa50cb5f2c89c578ebef925cffed5`). Page numbers are **PDF viewer pages** unless marked “printed”
(printed page = PDF page − 1). Evidence comes from each page's extracted text and a rendered contact sheet of all 75 pages.
The audit covers `app/resources/page.tsx`, `components/ResourceLibrary.tsx`, `components/ResourceDownload.tsx`,
`lib/resources.ts`, and every file in `public/resources/pdfs/2025/`.

## Executive summary

| Status | Count |
|---|---|
| Verified | 19 |
| Faithful paraphrase | 64 |
| Unsupported / needs review | 7 |
| **Items audited** | **90** (36 page-level items + 54 tools) |

**Downloads:** all 60 PDFs are present, linked, and served as `application/pdf`. None are missing, extra, or modified.
The full binder is byte-identical to the source. Each of the 59 cut files is pixel-identical to its source pages, in
order, and ends with the p. 75 credits page. No pages are blank.

> **Status:** content edits 1–7 below were applied on 2026-10-29 (see “Content edits”). Issue 1, permission, is still open.

**Highest-priority issues**
1. **Publication permission (highest).** The PDFs were committed and pushed on 2026-10-29 under the site owner's
   permission (`CLEARANCE` in `lib/resources.ts`). The binder's creators and several third-party rights holders have not
   given written authorization for public hosting or splitting. See the checklist below. This audit is not legal clearance.
2. **“Friendship” framing.** Two lines (“inside a real friendship”, “Tools work best with a friend”) blur the binder's own
   line between discipleship and friendship (p. 4).
3. **“Print them, share them”** reads as blanket permission, but p. 75 restricts reproduction for profit and routes
   reproduction questions to a named contact.
4. **The Equip overview** leaves out the binder's advice (p. 3) about when to begin that section.
5. **Page labels** show printed page numbers, which are one lower than the PDF viewer's.
6. **The Wheel's description** calls it “the Navigator picture”, which the binder page never says.
7. **Not from the binder:** “every student can meet one-on-one with a mentor” is a ministry claim; staff should confirm it.

**Fixed in this audit (clear-cut accuracy issues; nothing disputed):**
- the section download bar now mentions the appended credits page;
- the Alongsider credit now names *The Ways of the Alongsider* by Bill Mowry, as printed on p. 63;
- the Memorize God's Word credit now uses p. 22's wording.
